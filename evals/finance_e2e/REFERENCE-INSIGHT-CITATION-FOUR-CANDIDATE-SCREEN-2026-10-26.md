# Four citation-development candidates: preliminary source-row screen

This is a **non-independent diagnostic** of four previously seen-case responses
that passed the mechanical candidate gate in the [seven model-backed citation
development results](results/2026-10-26-reference-insight-citation-development-flash-v2-remaining7/).
It is not a full claim-by-claim F/I/R/S/U adjudication and does not change any
sealed result or assign `quality_pass=true`. Both final interpretations and
persisted review rationales were read against the frozen selected-row catalogs
and actual ledger state; full SEC-filing completeness and outside context were
not re-adjudicated here.

| Response | Selected-row cross-check | Open interpretation issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RELY r1 | Revenue rose; GAAP operating income and adjusted EBITDA fell; GAAP net income and EPS rose; tax expense also rose. The answer preserves this mixed direction and the held thesis. | The claim that the net-income improvement is associated with the other-income swing is consistent with the signed rows, but a complete causal account of the swing is unavailable. The response frames it as an accounting bridge rather than established business driver. |
| RELY r2 | The same opposing operating/headline signals and tax direction are represented correctly. The final and saved rationale identify the cost build and keep the ledger blocked. | “Driven by a swing in other income and by a higher pretax result” risks double-counting an included subtotal in prose. Treat this as a possible inference-discipline imprecision in full adjudication, not an automatically verified causal explanation. |
| TFC r2 | Earnings/EPS, noninterest income and average earning assets rose; provision and net chargeoffs fell; FTE margin slipped; noninterest expense and interest expense rose. The answer reflects these directions and the available ledger state. | Inferring that the FTE NII increase is associated with the larger average asset base rather than spread is *consistent* with these rows, but yields, funding mix and a full attribution bridge are absent. The answer's “driven by” language for reported earnings needs close review against the incomplete GAAP bridge. |
| VRSK r1 | GAAP and adjusted profitability declined while free cash flow rose. The frozen guidance ranges show revenue and adjusted EBITDA cut, adjusted EPS unchanged. The answer distinguishes guidance from realized results and keeps the ledger blocked. | Working-capital or other explanations for cash-flow divergence remain hypotheses only; the response mostly marks them as unresolved. The selected rows do not establish the persistence of the decline. |

The screen found no obvious wrong direction for the principal cited metrics in
these four candidates. That is **not** a high-quality count: each material
claim still needs an exact source/state pointer, complete arithmetic where
applicable, and F/I/R/S/U scoring under the frozen rubric. In particular, the
TFC attribution and RELY r2 subtotal wording show why a valid citation ID and
an empty semantic-alert list cannot be treated as proof of inference quality.
