# Source-distinct v4 SEC event insight-quality protocol

Status: **pre-observation protocol; no v4 model calls are authorized or counted.**
Freeze this file, the 20 source packets, fixture builder, runner, model routing,
Gateway binary and database template before any paid v4 observation. Any later
change requires a new arm; never silently pool it with v2/v3.

## Question and population

For 20 preselected issuer pairs with source-audited adjacent quarterly SEC
exhibits, can the runtime and Flash model repeatedly produce an accurate,
source-bounded *research-state insight* while correctly operating the thesis
and alert tools? This is not a test of investment returns, price forecasts,
trading recommendations or independent economic value. Cases were selected
before v4 model output; five fresh, isolated sessions per case yield 100
observations. The issuer pair, not a repeated session, is the reliability
unit. FDS is fiscal Q4→Q1; it is not labelled calendar Q2→Q3. NAVI uses the
same-filing detailed EX-99.2 exhibit rather than an announcement pointer.

## Intervention and execution

The intervention relative to the stopped v2 and complete v3 arms is a new,
source-distinct case frame with fuller typed facts and verified signed
accounting bridges. Host-computed differences and source-defined formulas
are supplied, but the prompt does not prescribe impact, conviction, or final
decision. The agent must read the stored thesis/alert, form its own bounded
implication, identify counterevidence and missing evidence, persist one
source-linked version-protected review, acknowledge the alert, and leave a
`needs_review` thesis blocked. A completed scripted-provider replay is a
tool-chain check only, not a model observation.

Before execution, record the randomized 100-item order, model alias and
provider version, input/output limits, max tool iterations, prices, binary and
template hashes, SEC source hashes, prompt/fixture/rubric hashes, spend cap,
and stop rules. The paid runner must require an explicit *new* authorization,
an operator-entered spend cap, and a fresh output directory. No failed
observation is retried or removed. Preserve request errors, timeouts, tool
budget failures, truncations, unpriced usage, partial state and raw traces;
stop the batch on a request/cost-integrity error or cap trigger. Distinguish
an account-balance interruption from a runtime defect, but count either as
incomplete in the primary denominator.

## Observation-level adjudication

Assess final prose **and** persisted review rationale against frozen source
exhibits, selected-row packets, and actual alert/thesis/trace state. The
source extract is incomplete by design: a claim that the *full filing* lacks
information cannot be inferred merely from absent selected rows. Quoted
management explanations are attributed statements, not automatically
independent causal proof. GAAP, adjusted/non-GAAP, reported operating
metrics, and forward guidance remain separate. Source-display rounding is
allowed only to the predeclared tolerance in the audited packet; it never
excuses a sign reversal or basis swap.

Enumerate every material claim—number, unit, period, comparison, formula,
driver, risk, uncertainty, decision and asserted tool state. Record exact
answer span, SEC row/URL/hash or trace/ledger pointer, recomputation where
applicable, and one of `supported`, `bounded_hypothesis`, `unsupported`,
`contradicted`, `unassessable`. Both omitted material counterevidence and
an avoidant claim that a fully supplied bridge is unknowable are errors.
An unassessable claim never becomes a pass by default.

Score F/I/R/S/U as 0, 1, 2 or truly nonapplicable, using the definitions in
`INSIGHT-QUALITY-HOLDOUT-RUBRIC-v2.md`:

- F: facts, units, periods, bases, arithmetic and source fidelity.
- I: supported implication versus unsupported causation or extrapolation.
- R: salient risks, contrary evidence, missing information and justified
  deferral.
- S: alignment of prose with actual review, alert, trace and thesis state.
- U: decision-useful research implication and next evidence condition,
  including a justified non-action outcome.

`high` requires all five dimensions 2, no critical error, mechanically clean
execution and an assessable implication. `adequate` requires every dimension
at least 1 and no critical error. Any 0 or critical error is `problematic`;
missing essential raw evidence is `not_assessable` and fails the primary
gate. A material wrong comparison, arithmetic sign, GAAP/non-GAAP conflation,
unsupported thesis-changing causal claim, false ledger state or actionable
recommendation without evidence is critical. A `needs_review` decision can
be high if the insight is substantive and the stored thesis is truly blocked;
generic refusal without using available evidence cannot score high.

Blind the judge to repetition/order and mechanical outcome while scoring
answer content, then reconcile the judgment with trace/ledger state. A
Codex/LLM judge is diagnostic, not independent finance-professional
validation. Do not call the result expert-approved without such a reviewer.

## Aggregation and claim boundary

A case passes only if **all five** sessions mechanically complete, none has
a critical error, at least four are `high`, and the fifth is no worse than
`adequate`. All 20 issuer cases must pass to support the predeclared strong
event-workflow claim. Report the observation-level score matrix, all
critical errors, case outcomes, and a one-sided exact binomial lower bound
over issuer cases; do not treat the 100 repeated sessions as independent
cases. Even 20/20 supports only a bounded claim for this SEC event→thesis
workflow and source/time frame, not universal financial insight quality.
Failure to meet the gate must be reported as such, with no post-hoc change
to the denominator, thresholds, source selection or prompt.

Source-distinct screening→candidate and portfolio-risk experiments with
their own frozen case frames and quality/state rubrics are required before
making a broader cross-workflow claim. Without independent domain review,
the defensible conclusion is about measured source-bounded operational
research quality, not investment value.
