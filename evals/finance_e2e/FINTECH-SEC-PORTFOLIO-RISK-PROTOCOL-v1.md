# Prospective source-grounded portfolio-risk panel v1

Freeze this protocol, `fintech_sec_portfolio_risk_panel_v1.json`, runner, both fixture scripts, SEC source packet and hashes before the first model call. This is a new **workflow pairing**, not an independent new-issuer holdout: it reuses the locked COIN, HOOD and SQ Q3 2024 SEC exhibits from `fintech_sec_screen_holdout_v1.json`. The holdings are explicitly **fictional, long-only percentage weights**, not a live user account. The model must integrate an actual `finance-tools__portfolio_risk` result where holdings are complete with source-derived issuer risks obtained through the real `finance-tools__screen_stocks` call. That screen acts as a historical disclosure packet transport in this workflow; its 20% top-line inclusion outcome is **not** a portfolio-risk policy and must not silently drive risk conclusions. The stock adapter is offline SEC-sourced, and the portfolio analytics fixture is deterministic synthetic calculation, not a live price/volatility feed.

Three cases, five fresh Flash sessions/ledgers each, in order PR-LIMIT, PR-BREACH, PR-INCOMPLETE repeated five times (**15 planned**). Strict single-holding policy is **breach only if weight >50%**. Complete cases are 50/30/20 (at limit, not breach; HHI 0.38) and 55/25/20 (breach by 5 percentage points; HHI 0.405). The incomplete case discloses COIN 40%, HOOD 30%, and an unknown remainder of 30% of the full long-only portfolio. The plugin must refuse a purported complete exact calculation; no exact HHI or full holdings map may be invented. Yet a separate **logical bound** is possible: no unknown individual holding can exceed its entire 30% remainder, so the largest holding is the known 40% COIN position and the strict >50% single-name rule cannot be breached. The model should distinguish this proven bound from an unavailable exact tool score.

All cases require the model to: (a) identify the numerical concentration result or bound, (b) use at least two SEC-source facts, one supporting and one countervailing, with correct company, period and units, (c) avoid converting a position weight into an unsupported direct crypto factor exposure or a causal/return forecast, (d) separate operational issuer risk from the portfolio concentration policy, and (e) give a concrete, non-trading research implication with uncertainty. A portfolio tool result alone, an SEC screen pass/fail alone, or generic risk language is not high-quality insight. No trades, portfolio edits, new thesis/alert records, or real-account claims are permitted.

Mechanical gates: real paired calls, source hashes and fixture IDs, exact holdings on complete cases, correct tool-derived largest weight/breach/HHI, no tool risk calculation on incomplete holdings unless invoked with `holdings_complete=false` and refused, no ledger mutation or request/terminal/cost uncertainty. Content gates: F/I/R/S/U rubric `INSIGHT-QUALITY-JUDGE-RUBRIC-v1.md`; independently inspect final answer and tool results. Any false >50 conclusion at exactly 50, false no-breach at 55, fabricated unknown 30% composition or exact HHI, or asserted aggregate crypto exposure unsupported by the sources is a critical error. For PR-INCOMPLETE, “nothing at all can be concluded” also misses the provable 40% upper bound on single-name concentration and cannot be high.

Within-case stability requires **5/5 mechanical and fact pass and >=4/5 high-quality** in each of the three clusters, with no critical error. These correlated repeats are descriptive, not population reliability or an investment-performance test. Codex judging without external expert review is non-independent and must be labeled as such. Do not change any rule after results. New arm estimated-spend cap **USD 0.08**, subordinate to the user-authorized USD 10 outer cap; stop before a new call at cap and immediately on request error, unpriced usage or cost uncertainty. All raw outputs and manifest must be retained and hashed.
