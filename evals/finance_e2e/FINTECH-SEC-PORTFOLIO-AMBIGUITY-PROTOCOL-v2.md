# Prospective issuer-aggregation ambiguity diagnostic v2

This is a **new diagnostic arm**, frozen before model calls. Do not overwrite or pool it with `FINTECH-SEC-PORTFOLIO-RISK-v1`: v1's incomplete-holdings prompt omitted an issuer-aggregation assumption, so its 40% bound was not a clean oracle. Keep the same real SEC-source, offline historical screen adapter and fictional long-only holdings, Flash model, 5,000 output-token ceiling, strict single-name breach rule `>50%`, fresh sessions and isolated finance ledgers. The selected COIN/HOOD/SQ Q3 2024 disclosures have appeared in prior experiments; this is not a new-issuer or new-source holdout.

Run five repetitions each for two explicitly opposed cases, alternating cases in repeat order (**10 observations**). `PA-DISTINCT` declares COIN 40% and HOOD 30% as final issuer-aggregated weights and the other 30% as distinct unidentified tickers. Largest position is exactly COIN 40%; no `>50%` breach can exist; exact HHI and the remaining tickers are unknown. `PA-OVERLAP` declares 40% and 30% as unaggregated observed line items and the 30% omnibus sleeve as possibly including more COIN or HOOD. COIN could then be 40–70%, HOOD 30–60%; a breach is **possible but not established**, and exact HHI is unknown. These alternatives are not to be mixed. The model should identify the case-specific logical policy conclusion without being told that conclusion in the user prompt.

Each response must call the actual `finance-tools__screen_stocks` historical SEC adapter for COIN, HOOD and SQ; inspect retained and rejected source facts; use at least two issuer-specific SEC facts as a supporting signal and counterpoint; distinguish issuer policy from the screen's 20% growth criterion; preserve periods, units and source; avoid fabricated residual composition, exact whole-portfolio HHI, factor exposures, return forecasts and trades. No new thesis, alert, account or transaction state. With incomplete holdings, not calling `finance-tools__portfolio_risk` is acceptable; a call with `holdings_complete=false` is acceptable **only if** the tool refuses and no exact score is claimed. Calling it with `true` is a mechanical failure.

Execution/trace gate: source-locked screen call, no ledger mutation, clean priced model/tool trace, no terminal or request error, and safe risk-tool behavior as just specified. Content gate: the existing F/I/R/S/U rubric, judged after results without changing the oracle. A false definite policy verdict in `PA-OVERLAP`, false uncertainty about the `PA-DISTINCT` no-breach bound, exact HHI invention, or claim that SQ is definitely absent from an unknown sleeve is an error. Within-case stability requires 5/5 mechanical and material fact passes and at least 4/5 high-quality answers in **both** cases, no critical error. Report the two clusters separately; correlated repetitions do not estimate population reliability. Codex judging is non-independent and does not validate investment value.

Stop before a new observation if the cap is reached; stop immediately on request error, unpriced usage or cost uncertainty. Preserve original results and manifest and hash them after run.
