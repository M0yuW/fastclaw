## Engineering change under test

The previous typed-answer arm passed the runtime/ledger checks in all eight sessions but held seven even after correcting an evidence-order evaluator defect. Its retained candidate records contain nine source-contradicted and eight unassessable numerical assertions. The v4 prompt already provided host-computed selected-row deltas; the model made errors while reconstructing and combining them in its own output.

The new answer contract instead asks the model to select IDs from a **complete** source-audited, host-derived comparison catalog. The host, not the model, renders every selected-row numeric comparison; source IDs, periods, SHA-256 values, units and basis stay attached. The model remains responsible for which facts matter, the implication, opposing evidence, uncertainty and the research-state decision. This change can reduce output arithmetic errors, but cannot itself establish correct causal interpretation, completeness or usefulness. A deliberately false qualitative direction can pass the deterministic schema gate and must be judged separately.

## Boundary before a quality claim

Conduct blinded F/I/R/S/U claim-level review of *both* the final interpretation and persisted review rationale against SEC rows and ledger state. The existing `INSIGHT-QUALITY-HOLDOUT-RUBRIC-v2.md` defines material errors and case-level labels. Codex or LLM-as-judge labels are diagnostic when no independent finance-domain reviewer is available; they are not expert validation. In particular, a citation ID cannot make an unsupported inference true.

For a prospective reliability claim, freeze a new source-distinct issuer/period frame, independently audit its SEC excerpts and periods, predeclare the model/runtime/plugin hashes, judgment rubric, cluster pass rule, random order, failure handling and stopping conditions **before** any new answer is observed. Keep event→review, screen→candidate and portfolio-risk workflows separate; seen-case development counts in none of those confirmatory denominators. A future report may claim only what its actual case diversity, judge independence and confidence interval support.
