# Same-case Mastercard tool-schema diagnostic v2: result

The post-run nested `causal_claims` schema repair was tested on five new, independent Flash sessions of the **same** frozen Mastercard packet. All five planned observations completed; all five had exactly one `thesis_record_review` call, zero rejected review calls, one persisted guarded review and an acknowledged alert. No tool-iteration terminal state, request error, unpriced usage or cost uncertainty was observed. Estimated model spend was **USD 0.009363872** against the diagnostic's USD 0.05 stop; this is not a provider invoice. `RESULT-SHA256.txt` verifies the manifest and all five raw `result.json` files. The original v1 Mastercard 3/5 completion and two failures remain unchanged.

This is positive evidence for a narrower **tool-schema usability** fix: publishing the required nested fields and allowed `strength` values was followed by first-attempt valid review calls in this same-case diagnostic. It is not a randomized causal estimate, a new-issuer holdout or proof of general recovery reliability.

The separate Codex F/I/R/S/U answer audit is less favorable: **0 high, 3 adequate, 2 problematic**. `r3` introduced `MA-Q2-ADJ-OPEX-YOY=12%` as if present in the selected baseline even though that fact was not supplied; the [actual Q2 SEC release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139124000159/ma06302024-exx991xearnings.htm) reports 9% adjusted operating-expense YoY growth. `r5` correctly cited the Q2 12% and Q3 25% **GAAP** YoY rates but wrongly said rates reported in successive quarters cannot be compared as a growth-rate trend. The [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139124000200/ma09302024-exx991xearnings.htm) confirms 25%. Neither answer's final prose is fully protected by the ledger-only causal-claim validator. Individual ratings and reasons are in `INSIGHT-JUDGE-v1.csv`; the judge is not blinded or a human expert.

Thus the repair cleared the observed **schema-induced workflow failures** but did **not** establish stable high-quality financial reasoning. The broad project goal remains open. The next experiment should use previously unseen real-source cases across more than the event-review workflow, with explicit source packets, pre-frozen judgment rules, and the same separate execution/content denominators. Do not pool these five same-case diagnostic runs with the v1 holdout's 15 observations or replace its negative findings.
