# Intel SEC charge-composition retest: result

This is the five-repeat, source-completeness treatment frozen in `../../INTEL-CHARGE-BRIDGE-RETEST-PROTOCOL-v1.md`. It is separate from and does not rewrite the earlier four-case SEC event panel. The original Intel Q2→Q3 2024 baseline, four Q3 event metrics, Flash model, finance tools and review prompt were kept. The Q3 event packet additionally exposed the locked SEC exhibit's USD 3.1 billion manufacturing-asset charge (substantially all in cost of sales), USD 2.9 billion goodwill/intangible impairment, and USD 9.9 billion deferred-tax-asset valuation allowance, which sum to the USD 15.9 billion bundle; USD 2.8 billion restructuring is separate. The Gateway binary also includes the production startup-readiness fix, so this is a descriptive source-completeness contrast, not a randomized single-factor causal trial.

All **5/5** observations completed real review and alert tools with source and observed-metric retention; all five original `result.json` hashes verify against `RESULT-SHA256.txt`. There were no request errors or uncertain-cost sequences. Estimated token cost was **USD 0.0118834632**, under the USD 0.03 cap.

The Codex first-pass judge (`INSIGHT-JUDGE-v1.csv`) found **2 high, 1 adequate, 2 problematic**. The two problematic observations are decision-relevant accounting inferences despite correct execution and basic arithmetic:

- `H-INTC-BRIDGE-r1`: persisted review describes margin collapse as attributable to the USD 3.1 billion manufacturing-asset item **plus** USD 2.9 billion goodwill and USD 9.9 billion tax-allowance items. Only the manufacturing-asset item is identified by the source as substantially in cost of sales. The full USD 15.9 billion bundle cannot be assigned to gross margin.
- `H-INTC-BRIDGE-r5`: final answer says reported gross margin is not a clean read because the charge composition includes a deferred-tax-asset valuation allowance. The tax allowance is not a gross-margin item. The 3.1 billion cost-of-sales charge can affect the reported margin, but the tax allowance does not.

The other three observations separated the charge categories and downgraded conviction in view of the 20.4-percentage-point GAAP margin decline, while noting missing Q3 operating/segment information. `r3` still loosely grouped the thesis's separate “material margin deterioration” and “sustained segment weakness” terms, so it is adequate rather than high.

The protocol's targeted stability criterion (at least 4/5 sound inference/risk and utility, no critical error) **was not met**. Providing complete primary-source charge composition did not reliably remove the inference error. This narrows the next engineering question: the agent needs a general accounting-lineage or claim-support check before persisting a causal explanation, not only more raw facts. This five-run single-company test does not establish a rate of failure in other issuers. The judge was unblinded, single-model, and not independently adjudicated; the labels are exploratory. No investment-value or return claim follows.
