The packet intentionally avoids a complete financial thesis, valuation, market price, future return and current-account information. The audit oracle permits a bounded interpretation of the stated changes, but not a causal claim that credit quality, segment profitability, or future investment merit is proven by the selected figures. The runtime test must preserve this distinction between what the SEC exhibit says, what the packet selected, and what the model inferred.
