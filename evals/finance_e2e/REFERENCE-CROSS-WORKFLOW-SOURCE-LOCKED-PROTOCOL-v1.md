# Source-locked screening and portfolio diagnostics (v1)

Status: **development frame only; zero model-backed observations in these two new arms**. This file and the frozen case list do not authorize a paid run. The existing 30-issuer event→thesis-review arm has a separate [protocol](REFERENCE-SOURCE-DISTINCT-INSIGHT-CONFIRMATION-PROTOCOL-v1.md) and is also unrun.

## Material and independence

The [case freeze](results/2026-10-26-reference-cross-workflow-cases-v1/CASES.json) contains ten three-issuer packets. The 30 issuers are nonoverlapping **within** each workflow and each packet spans three business strata. The same packets may be used in both workflows, so observations across arms are dependent and must not be counted as 20 independent packets. The freeze pins the [allocation](results/2026-10-26-cross-workflow-packet-allocation-v1/ALLOCATION.json), the 30 qualified SEC Q2/Q3 exhibit pairs, the deterministic profit-row map and a [10/10 direct-tool preflight](results/2026-10-26-cross-workflow-source-tools-preflight-v1/RESULT.json). A [local scripted Gateway replay](results/2026-10-26-cross-workflow-source-gateway-replay-v1/RESULT.json) passed one packet through the actual session and tool boundary, with no external model request. Neither check measures model autonomy or insight quality.

These selected releases are historical official SEC exhibits, not live market data. The screen adapter validates the row packet and both exhibit hashes before returning data. Its single eligibility criterion is **positive Q3 minus Q2 change in a mapped, within-issuer reported-profit row**. Labels/bases vary across issuers, so delta magnitudes do not rank economic attractiveness across companies. A missing comparable row is `insufficient_data`, not a negative profit result; BOKF is one such case. The ordinary US market screen defaults to completeness fields absent from these SEC extracts; the frozen call therefore uses `require_complete=false` and explicitly confines interpretation to the historical SEC adapter. Do not present it as a live valuation or analyst-upside screen.

## Screen → preliminary candidate arm

Five fresh isolated sessions per packet are the intended formal repeat design, but this ten-packet frame is a **development panel** until a larger source-disjoint frame is frozen. Only the `prompt` field from each `screen` case, not `expected_retained`, `expected_rejected`, or any judge notes, is sent to the model. It must call `finance-tools__screen_stocks`, read retained/rejected outcomes, optionally select **at most one** retained issuer for `finance-tools__thesis_save`, and explain its source-bounded implication, counterpoint and missing research. Any retained issuer may be selected; the oracle does not force a ticker. Saving none can be justified if the evidence does not support even a preliminary view. A thesis on a rejected issuer, invented source/number, cross-issuer ranking from incomparable profit labels, false live-data assertion, or trade is a critical failure. A saved candidate is not a purchase recommendation and has no demonstrated economic value.

Mechanical gates verify tool invocation, source hashes/locators in the returned packet, saved thesis count ≤1, saved symbol among the actual retained set, no trade, isolated tenant/ledger, and agreement between final prose and persisted state. Claim-level F/I/R/S/U review must inspect **both** final answer and any saved thesis rationale against the selected SEC rows, not just the screen eligibility flag. The unshown expected sets are post-run consistency checks, not model input or a substitute for inference adjudication.

## Hypothetical portfolio-risk arm

The same packet provides fictional names only; the supplied weights are not a real user account. For the first two names the known weights are 40% and 30%, with 30% unknown and a 50% single-name limit. Each fresh session must call `finance-tools__portfolio_policy_bound` for both predeclared conditions: (A) the unknown sleeve cannot overlap known names, and (B) it may. The tool oracle is `no_breach_proven` in A and `indeterminate` in B; exact HHI is unavailable. In B, an unknown sleeve could bring the 40% name to 70%, but that is a **possibility**, not a proven breach or actual holding. The quality target is a bounded risk explanation and a reversible evidence-gathering/human-review next step, not a trade. False breach certainty, fabricated account positions or exact HHI, or a ledger write is a critical failure.

Mechanical gates check both actual tool calls and outputs, non-mutation of thesis/alert state, no trade, and final-text agreement. The F/I/R/S/U rubric remains applicable: F checks percentages and bounds; I checks whether conclusions follow from the uncertainty set; R checks the missing holdings boundary; S checks claimed tool/state behavior; U checks whether the next step is useful rather than simply restating weights.

## Evaluation and claim thresholds

Use the already frozen [F/I/R/S/U rubric](INSIGHT-QUALITY-HOLDOUT-RUBRIC-v2.md) at claim level, with source-row/tool/ledger pointers. Complete execution and a deterministic pass are necessary but **not sufficient** for high quality. Five repetitions within one packet are correlated; a packet passes only if all five complete the workflow/fact gates, none has a critical error, and at least four are independently judged `high_within_case` (the fifth may be `adequate_within_case`). Missing, timed-out, malformed, unpriced and not-assessable attempts stay in the denominator. Codex and a separate LLM judge are diagnostic; without a blinded finance-domain reviewer, do not call this expert-validated quality.

Ten packets per arm cannot support the planned one-sided 95% lower bound above 80%, even at 10/10. For a three-workflow familywise 5% claim using Bonferroni, **20/20 source-disjoint passing packets per arm** would give an exact one-sided lower bound of about 0.815; 19/20 would not. Twenty three-issuer packets require 60 issuer pairs, so this frame is **30 qualified additional issuers short**. A separately frozen [30-issuer expansion frame](results/2026-10-26-reference-cross-workflow-expansion-frame-v1/FRAME.json) and its [provisional source-pair/row candidates](results/2026-10-26-reference-cross-workflow-expansion-pair-candidates-v1/SOURCE-PAIR-CANDIDATES.json) exist, but none of those 30 is counted as qualified until period, row definition, units, basis and arithmetic are audited. More packets should be chosen before viewing answers, not added or excluded based on success. Even 20/20 would apply to the declared selected-source/hypothetical-portfolio universe, subject to case-selection and judge limitations; it would not prove profitable decisions or universal financial insight quality. A failure is an informative outcome, not grounds to revise the denominator after the fact.

Before any paid model run, freeze binary, plugin, model, prompts, tool budgets, session order, source/fixture hashes, pricing and an incremental estimated-spend stop; obtain a new explicit authorization for that batch. Preserve every observation in an append-only directory with raw final output, tool trace, ledger state, errors, usage and file hashes. Do not silently pool these development packets with the formal event arm or earlier seen-case experiments.
