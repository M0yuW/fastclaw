# Four-workflow extension, v2 (exploratory)

Eight assigned isolated workflow attempts were completed: four workflows, two repetitions each, in the frozen order recorded in `manifest.json`. Each attempt used a fresh Gateway home, model session, and finance ledger. The agent policy permitted only finance research, screen, and risk tools; it denied shell, direct network tools, model-driven event ingestion, and trading. W01 used SHA-256-locked SEC packets. W02 used two claims in one explicitly synthetic fixture document. W03/W04 used deterministic synthetic scripts, not live market data.

| Workflow | Attempts | Prespecified checks | Independent semantic reading |
|---|---:|---|---|
| W01: NVIDIA Q1→Q2→Q4 research reviews | 2 | 2/2: two alerts, two linked reviews, version 1→3, both alerts acknowledged, baseline 26,044 retained, Q1→Q4 not called QoQ | Both preserved growth and margin risk. W01-r1 wrote Q1→Q2 revenue growth as 15.35%; exact inputs yield 15.343...%, or 15.34% to two decimals. W01-r2 wrote 15.34%. This small arithmetic error is not a state-transition failure. |
| W02: conflicting capex statements in one synthetic fixture | 2 | 2/2: both claims retained, `needs_review`, status/conviction unchanged, source hash linked, alert acknowledged | The two statements had distinct fact IDs but shared one fixture-file hash. This is a claim-conflict test, not yet an independent-source provenance test. |
| W03: screen → persisted candidate thesis | 2 | 2/2: FICC retained; FICD rejected for missing earnings growth; only FICC thesis created with P/E 16.5, free cash flow 310, ROIC 0.13 and synthetic provenance in the persisted text | The prior pilot's omission of numeric values from the ledger did not recur under the more explicit task requirement. This does not prove general transfer. |
| W04: no-breach concentration diagnostic | 2 | 2/2: risk tool invoked; largest holding 45% versus 50% limit; no false breach, trade, or ledger mutation | Both reports described a compliant synthetic portfolio. |

All 8/8 completed without request/provider errors and met the declared mechanical checks. There were 10 model-backed turns, 34 priced model calls, 120.931 seconds of summed Gateway wall time, and USD 0.036750 in frozen-price estimated model cost. The USD 0.10 cap did not trigger. Independent SQLite inspection matched the expected records: W01 had one thesis/two reviews/two alerts per attempt; W02 one/one/one; W03 one thesis only; W04 no state records. Source and code hashes in the manifest still match; the isolated Gateway port is closed. These heterogeneous, selected cases do not support a population reliability estimate.

The follow-up independent-source experiment is recorded separately in `../2026-10-17-finance-conflict-two-sources-v1/`. It must not be substituted into W02 or pooled with these scores. In particular, the finance plugin's current deterministic guard covers *missing required metrics*, not disagreement between two complete sources. That distinction matters to the safety claim.
