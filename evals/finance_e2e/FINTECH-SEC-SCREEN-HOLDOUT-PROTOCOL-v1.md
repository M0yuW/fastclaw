# Prospective FinTech SEC screening-to-thesis holdout v1

Freeze this protocol, JSON corpus, offline adapter, runner and all raw SEC exhibit hashes **before** any model observation. This complements the event-to-thesis holdout; it must not replace or be pooled with failed earlier observations. Coinbase, Robinhood and Block are new issuer/episode configurations for this screen, not evidence that the model has never encountered these public filings in training.

## Workflow and source boundary

Each of three Q3 2024 SEC-furnished earnings exhibits is mapped into the existing `finance-tools__screen_stocks` tool by a test-only offline adapter that verifies raw source SHA-256 on every invocation. The adapter supplies reported fields and deterministic inclusion/exclusion only; it supplies **no risk note, thesis, counterpoint or investment conclusion**. The model sees one issuer per request and must derive a material counterpoint from source-derived figures, explain the criterion decision, and, only when retained, persist one historical preliminary candidate thesis with source URL/hash, period, numbers, risk, and explicit valuation/price boundary. No live-price call or trade is allowed. The screen cannot establish contemporaneous peer ranking because release dates and revenue labels vary.

The single predeclared rule is issuer-reported top-line revenue YoY growth **at least 20%**. COIN's current/prior-year total revenue 1,205,193/674,148 USD thousand yields `(1,205,193 / 674,148 - 1) * 100 = 78.77276…%`, rounded to 78.8%. HOOD reports total net revenues +36%. Both pass. SQ reports total net revenue +6% and fails. SQ's gross profit +19% and revenue excluding bitcoin +11% may be discussed but must **not be substituted** for top-line revenue growth to force a pass. These are historical research screen rules, not evidence of attractiveness or a comparative valuation. The model should distinguish COIN's strong YoY total revenue from its disclosed -17% QoQ revenue and -27% QoQ transaction revenue, HOOD's +36% total net revenue from the prior-year regulatory-accrual comparator and crypto mix, and SQ's strong gross profit from its failing top-line criterion. The exact wording of a useful counterpoint is not prescribed to the model.

Official source exhibits: [COIN Q3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978824000186/q324shareholderletter.htm), [HOOD Q3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1783879/000178387924000295/q32024robinhoodexhibit991.htm), [SQ Q3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512673/000119312524253135/d835206dex991.htm). `fintech_sec_screen_holdout_v1.json` freezes selected field labels, units, dates, locators, URLs and byte hashes. This is a selected source packet, not the full filing made available to the agent; conclusions about an omitted detail must be phrased as packet-level only.

## Planned observations and gates

Five fresh independent Flash sessions and ledgers per issuer: **15 observations**, order COIN, HOOD, SQ repeated five times. No replacement runs; request errors, terminal failures and malformed tool calls remain in the denominator. The existing F/I/R/S/U rubric is fixed before scoring, and the judge must inspect final text **and** persisted state. The same Codex author as judge is non-blind, not an independent finance expert; score accordingly and disclose that limitation.

1. **Source/fact:** exact raw-source hash; correct date, period, unit and revenue label; COIN derived growth 78.8% within 0.1 point; no confusion of reported YoY rate with sequential growth; no false assertion that positive gross profit or income satisfies the top-line rule.
2. **Tool/state:** paired `screen_stocks` call with expected result; one persisted source-linked historical preliminary thesis for COIN/HOOD or zero for SQ; no unexpected alert, trade, stale review date, request/terminal error or uncertain spend. Prose simulation of a tool call fails.
3. **Inference/risk:** for retained issuers, identify at least one material counterpoint from **different** supplied fields and explain how it limits the preliminary view. For SQ, explain failed +6% criterion and why +19% gross profit does not rescue it under this rule; nevertheless recognize that gross profit growth warrants separate investigation. Do not make a causal assertion that the packet cannot support, or claim missing price/valuation evidence implies negative fundamental quality.
4. **Research utility:** a high label needs a concise, source-grounded implication and discriminating follow-up question, not only a restatement of tool output or generic “do more research.” A no-thesis rejection can be high when it correctly explains the precise rule boundary and useful alternate research path.

The stringent descriptive stability gate is 5/5 execution and material-fact pass **and** at least 4/5 high-quality answers in **each** issuer cluster with no critical error. This is a small within-case gate, not a population reliability estimate. Report per issuer and raw counts even if it fails; do not alter the gate, source packet or prompt after observing answers. Source/adapter/template/model/runner/plugin/rubric hashes and exact prompt are recorded in the manifest.
