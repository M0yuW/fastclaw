The new Upstart field prevents a misleading conclusion that the sequential narrowing of GAAP net loss is entirely operating improvement. Removing that gain from Q3's reported net loss gives a simple **illustrative** -40,119 USD thousand, not an issuer-endorsed adjusted earnings metric. It must not be silently presented as GAAP or a full normalization; other nonrecurring items and tax effects are not evaluated by this one subtraction.

The official IR cross-check supports the draft transcriptions and exposes the added Upstart interpretive caveat. It does **not** prove that SEC-hosted bytes are identical to the issuer-hosted PDFs, that every selected fact appears in the SEC exhibit, or that the whole source corpus is frozen. Before any prospective paid model observation, obtain and hash the six SEC exhibits (or explicitly create a separate, numbered IR-source protocol and raw-byte lock), run the source preflight and a semantic fact-to-source audit, then freeze code, prompts, rubric, template and binary. Do not call the current draft a validated holdout result.
