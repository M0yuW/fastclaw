# Evidence needed for a stable financial-insight claim

## Current conclusion

The archived three-issuer fresh-SEC panel had 0/15 `high_within_case` answers, 14/15 mechanical completions, and two problematic answers under the frozen F/I/R/S/U rubric. This is adverse evidence for the present configuration, not a sample-size problem that can be fixed by reinterpreting the same runs. The five repeats of each issuer share one fact packet and decision trap; the independent scenario count is at most three, not fifteen. The ten-answer independent Flash claim-checker diagnostic missed four of five first-pass-labeled material issues, so it cannot replace source-based claim adjudication. Neither of these results is changed by later deterministic plugin fixes.

## Two different success statements

1. **Panel consistency:** Within a preselected case, all required tools and ledger transitions complete, every material figure/basis is correct, no critical error occurs, and at least four of five fresh repetitions are `high_within_case`. Passing a few such cases supports only a statement about those cases.
2. **Cross-case reliability:** The fraction of previously unseen, source-locked case clusters that meet the entire panel-consistency rule is high within each claimed workflow. An issuer/event cluster, not a repeated model answer, is the sampling unit. Event-to-thesis, screening-to-candidate and portfolio-risk workflows must be reported separately before a claim spanning them. A no-trade or justified deferral can pass; investment returns are not the outcome.

For an operational, not investment-value, reliability claim, predeclare a target of at least 80% case-level success with a one-sided 95% exact binomial lower bound above 80% **for each workflow**. With all cases succeeding, the lower bound is `0.05^(1/n)`, so at least 14 independent case clusters are needed per workflow for a separate 95% statement. If making a simultaneous claim for three workflows and allocating familywise alpha `0.05/3`, at least 19 all-success clusters per workflow are needed (`(0.05/3)^(1/19) ≈ 0.806`). These are mathematical minima under independent, exchangeable case sampling; failures require more cases, and hand-picked examples invalidate population-style inference. Five repeats per cluster would make the minimal three-workflow all-success design 285 model observations. This is a planning bound, **not** authorization or a commitment to run 285 paid calls.

## Prospective execution conditions

- First use the existing three-new-issuer FinTech draft as a small diagnostic only after its six official source files are locally cached, byte-hashed, semantically audited and frozen before any model output. It cannot establish cross-workflow stability by itself.
- Before a larger arm, freeze a source-diverse sampling frame for each workflow, including positive, mixed, adverse, missing and conflicting evidence. No same-issuer/near-duplicate packet should masquerade as another independent case. Fix model, runtime, prompt, tools, budget, judge rubric and error-denominator rules per arm; do not replace failed observations.
- Judge the final prose **and** persisted ledger state against the exact source packet. Record claim-level source pointers, arithmetic and basis checks, critical errors and F/I/R/S/U scores. The existing Codex first pass is not an independent expert gold standard; the weak Flash checker cannot silently adjudicate. Report unresolved judgments as such and do not count them as high quality.
- Report mechanical completion, factual correctness, inference discipline, research utility and case-level consistency separately. State the exact claim boundary: repeatable, source-bounded research explanations in the tested workflows, not profitable advice or superior investment judgment. An external blinded finance review is still needed for a strong claim about the subjective value of the insights.

This plan is deliberately stronger than the 3-cluster × 5-repeat diagnostic gate. It cannot turn the existing failing panel into positive evidence; it identifies the actual new observations and adjudication needed to test the user's broader objective.
