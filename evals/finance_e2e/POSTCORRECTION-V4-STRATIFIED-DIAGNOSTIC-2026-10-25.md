This fixed algorithm uses neither the answer nor the private run-order/mechanical mapping. Review the final prose, persisted review, source packet and tool/ledger evidence; record concrete supported and contradicted claims, distinguishing source arithmetic from causal inference. The reviewer here is Codex, not an independent finance professional. ## Diagnostic synthesis and repair implications

All 20 fixed selections have now been read. **Eight** contain at least one exact, source- or arithmetic-checkable wrong line, sign, rank, direction or magnitude (EVTC, FITB, MKTX, NTRS, RELY, SEIC, USB, VRSK). LPLA separately contradicts a supplied exact bridge by claiming a reconciliation is unavailable. RKT and TFC make dominant-driver or margin-mechanism inferences that exceed the selected evidence. HBAN exposes a fixture omission: the model was not given the packet's `notable_item` block. Seven selected answers have no material contradiction identified in this focused read (C, FDS, FLYW, NAVI, SPGI, TRU, WEX), and CPAY needs clearer relative-versus-absolute wording. These categories describe this **diagnostic one-per-issuer sample only**; no extrapolated 100-answer pass rate or formal F/I/R/S/U score is asserted.

The error pattern is not primarily SEC byte retrieval. The audited source rows and host-supplied signed bridges are often correct, while the final prose mislabels a line, reverses a comparison, infers a cause from an identity, or describes a saved state inaccurately. A future arm should (1) carry all material typed packet fields into the prompt, including HBAN's notable item, and audit the rendered prompt; (2) verify final-answer numerical comparisons and signed bridge claims against host facts before treating a run as complete; (3) verify claims about persisted evidence, next-review date and hold state against the ledger, not merely tool-call success; and (4) make the review-tool contract and error recovery explicit, particularly around optional `causal_claims`, required `thesis_id`, and JSON-like sibling fields swallowed into rationale. These are engineering-development steps. Any post-fix confirmatory panel needs a new source-distinct frame and separate paid authorization; v4 remains sealed and failed under its own gate.
