The first two are model-output reasoning errors despite source-locked inputs, not SEC source-cell errors. The third is an end-to-end decision/state inconsistency. The raw records also contain weaker, potentially misleading inferences that need line-by-line adjudication—for example GS-r4's claim that earnings improvement "rests on" a revenue mix shift and lower provision without a reconciled earnings bridge, and OMF-r5's inference that lower charge-off **ratio** establishes lower realized loss emergence without charge-off dollars or a stable denominator. These are **review flags**, not counted here as confirmed critical errors.

Further targeted reading found a recurring *coverage* issue, still pending full F/I/R/S/U adjudication: BNY-r1/r3/r4/r5 describe the revenue increase as coming from, driven by, or predominantly accounted for by NII. The selected figures are total revenue +53, NII +33, and fee revenue −4 USD million, leaving **+24 USD million of unlisted revenue change** (45.3% of the total increase). In particular, BNY-r4's final answer calls that residual "small". The direction of NII and fee movement is supported, but any exhaustive or causal explanation of total revenue from those two lines is not. This flag is separate from BNY-r2's already confirmed arithmetic contradiction and must not be silently counted as four more critical failures before individual judgment.

OMF-r3 and r5 describe a lower *realized-loss* outcome from a lower **net charge-off ratio** (7.19% → 6.67%) without net charge-off dollars or comparable balance denominators. A lower ratio is directly supported; a lower absolute loss amount is not established. Both answers also request denominator data, so the final judgment must examine their precise qualifier rather than treating the warning or the claim in isolation. GS-r4's final "earnings improvement rests on a mix shift plus a lower provision" is likewise a pending inference flag, because the selected GS revenue components leave a +1001 USD million unlisted revenue residual and the packet does not bridge net earnings. These observations show why review of **both** persisted rationale and final prose matters.

### Prospective engineering correction after the freeze

The FISV-r3 mismatch was traced to the finance plugin's hold rule: it set `decision_hold` only for missing required metrics or stored source conflicts, not for a voluntary `needs_review` decision. The plugin now requires a linked alert for `needs_review`, forbids status/conviction changes, and blocks actionability until a complete, conflict-free same-event alert with source continuity and new evidence permits a substantive review. A focused regression test and the full plugin suite pass. This correction was made **after** the 25 observations; it does not retroactively change or re-score FISV-r3. The GS and BNY reasoning errors are not fixed by this state guard.

An offline `selected_fact_delta_guard_v1.py` now derives same-metric changes and explicitly reports unlisted revenue residuals from audited selected-fact packets. It shows BNY's +53 revenue change is **not** exhausted by NII +33 and fees −4 (unlisted residual +24), and GS's +601 total revenue change is **not** exhausted by market-making −865 and investment banking +465 (unlisted residual +1001). It also computes the GS earnings and revenue changes as +375 and +601, respectively. This is a reproducible post-run check and a possible *future* intervention; it was not part of the frozen 25 model sessions and does not repair their answers.

## What this can and cannot establish

The three confirmed exceptions already prevent the GS, BNY, and FISV five-session clusters from satisfying the predeclared all-five fact/state gate, regardless of how their other sessions score. MCO and OMF remain subject to full conclusion-level adjudication. Therefore this first batch **does not demonstrate stable high-quality insight generation**, even though all sessions finished and most tool/ledger checks passed. It does demonstrate that the isolated runtime can carry the source-bound event→alert→review→acknowledgement chain through 25 paid sessions at the recorded estimated cost, with a visible failure mode rather than hidden retries.

The next quality pass must enumerate and classify *every material claim* in both final prose and persisted review, attach exact SEC fact IDs/arithmetic or tool-state pointers, then score F/I/R/S/U per observation. Because the present reviewer is unblinded and no independent finance-domain reviewer is available, even that pass will be a transparent diagnostic judgment, not independent expert validation. No additional paid run is authorized by this memo.
