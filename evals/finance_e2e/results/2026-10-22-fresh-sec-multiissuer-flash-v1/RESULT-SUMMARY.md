# Fresh SEC multi-issuer forward panel v1: first-pass result

The source-and-rubric protocol was frozen before model execution. Six SEC-furnished earnings-release exhibits for previously unused issuers AAPL, MSFT and SBUX were cached and byte-hashed. Fifteen independent Flash sessions ran in a fixed randomized order (five repeats per case), using actual finance-tool ingestion, guarded review and ledger state. No archived model outputs were included in prompts. Estimated model spend was **$0.034464** against a **$0.10** stop cap; this is usage-based estimation, not a provider invoice.

| Case cluster | Mechanical completion | High within case | Adequate | Problematic | Main quality signal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| AAPL tax/earnings basis | 5/5 | 0 | 4 | 1 | All runs kept GAAP and tax-adjusted EPS distinct, but several wrongly treated adjacent-quarter figures as lacking a valid sequential comparison. |
| MSFT cloud level versus rate | 5/5 | 0 | 5 | 0 | All distinguished rising absolute cloud sales from lower YoY growth percentages; insight was usually a careful restatement. |
| SBUX traffic/margin deterioration | 4/5 | 0 | 4 | 1 | Four useful downgrades, but repeated wording mixed YoY rates with GAAP margin levels; one run exhausted tool iterations without persisting a review. |
| **Total** | **14/15** | **0** | **13** | **2** | **The preregistered panel-consistency gate failed.** |

The mechanical failure is `FRESH-SBUX-TRAFFIC-r4`: its transport and usage were priced, but successive `thesis_record_review` calls submitted unsupported causal target names. The guard rejected them with `unsupported_causal_claim`; one call had invalid JSON. The run ended at `tool_iteration_budget_exhausted`, leaving thesis version 1, no review, and an unacknowledged alert. This is a model/tool-schema recovery failure, not a DeepSeek transport timeout or account-balance event. The guard prevented an unsupported ledger claim but did not guide the agent to an accepted review within the configured budget. The observation remains in all denominators and was not rerun.

The **content audit is distinct from execution**. The case facts and comparison bases were checked against the cached SEC exhibits and the frozen extracted-fact audit. Codex then reviewed each final response with its persisted review, alert and thesis state under the pre-existing F/I/R/S/U rubric. The individual scores and reasons are in `INSIGHT-JUDGE-v1.csv`. `FRESH-AAPL-TAX-r2` is problematic because it asserts that adjacent Q3/Q4 figures permit no sequential inference, even though same-basis revenue and GAAP EPS levels are supplied; the caution could suppress a valid research interpretation. Other answers avoid fabricated cross-statement causality, yet often add little beyond input restatement and overstate what `statement_line=unknown` means. No predictive return, trading merit or future investment value was tested.

The preregistered gate required each issuer cluster to have at least 4/5 high-quality answers, all 15 mechanically complete, no critical errors and F=2 in all 15. It failed on each of those criteria. **This result contradicts a broad claim that the current runtime stably produces high-quality financial insight** under this panel's strict definition. A supportable narrower claim is that the runtime completed most source-grounded review workflows, preserved auditable state, and rejected unsupported persisted causality; the final narrative and error-recovery interface still need improvement. These are five correlated repeats of only three selected issuers, judged by Codex alone, so the observed counts are descriptive, not a population reliability estimate.

Raw `result.json` and `manifest.json` files are frozen in `RESULT-SHA256.txt`; the source corpus records hashes of all six original HTML exhibits. No raw result was edited after its hash was recorded. To verify from repository root: `shasum -a 256 -c evals/finance_e2e/results/2026-10-22-fresh-sec-multiissuer-flash-v1/RESULT-SHA256.txt`.
